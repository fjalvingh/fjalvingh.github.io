# Computers and Calculators

The desktop sized machines: the programmable calculators that were really
early desktop computers, and the portables that came after them.

- [HP 9825A](hp-9825a/index.md) - a 16 bit calculator from 1976, with a CPU
  that is a work of art.
- [HP 85A](hp-85a/index.md) - an 8 bit calculator with a built in CRT and tape
  drive, 1980, and its repair.
- [Dolch Pac 64](dolch-pac-64/index.md) - a luggable PC with a full set of ISA
  slots.
