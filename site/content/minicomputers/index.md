# MiniComputers

The minicomputers in the collection: the machines from the era when a computer
was a cabinet full of boards, and every one of them can be repaired with a
scope and the original engineering drawings.

- [PDP Common info](pdp-common-info/index.md) - connectors, board lists, file
  formats and diagnostics that are not specific to one machine.
- [The PDP 11/10](pdp-1110/index.md) - one of the earliest PDP-11's (1972), the
  same machine as the 11/05, in a 5 1/4" box.
- [PDP-11/44](pdp-1144/index.md) - a Unibus 11/44, and by now the largest pile
  of notes here: boards, backplanes, diagnostics and the software to run on it.
- [Peripherals](peripherals/index.md) - the drives and other peripherals that
  hang off these machines.
- [The Unibone](unibone/index.md) - the Unibone/QBone device emulator, and
  everything done with it.
- [The HP 1000 (2100 E-series 2113B)](hp1000/index.md) - a 16 bit HP
  minicomputer from around 1976, complete in a rack with its disk subsystem and
  paper tape reader.
